# Five-minute demonstration

Use only seeded local fictitious data. Open the shopkeeper app and a separate customer browser; local private URLs are written by the seed to `.demo-session.json` or can be reissued through the app.

1. Show Dashboard metrics. Explain that a quotation is a proposal, not received money.
2. Open a Case A ticket. Show initial condition, accessories and the handover receipt. Explain the difference between the ticket number, private tracking link and separate collection code.
3. Issue a quotation from Checking. Open the customer link, inspect the itemized total and confirm an explicit decision. Refresh the owner app, then start repair. The customer does not install an app.
4. Open the revised-quote example. Earlier approval applies to its earlier version only; revised work waits for another decision.
5. Open the Case B family link. Show that prices, photos, quotations, receipts and approval controls are absent. The family recipient only passes permitted status/pickup information.
6. Open Case C. It has no contact number. Print the handover receipt and use the agreed in-person return time.
7. Open a ready-for-return example. It remains in the shop's custody until the collection wizard records actual handover. An unrepaired return has no new repair warranty.
8. Open a repaired Delivered record. Show itemized charges, recorded payment, remaining balance and precise covered work/exclusions. Warranty comes from the repair shop.

Acceptance goals are a basic intake within two minutes and an online customer refresh within five seconds under documented conditions. The source includes these goals but does not claim a timed human-intake result or mobile-network performance guarantee.
