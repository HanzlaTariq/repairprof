# Outside this MVP

Automated SMS/WhatsApp, push notifications, customer accounts/apps, subscriptions, online payment processing, multiple staff/branches, persistent offline drafts, AI diagnosis and device-location tracking are intentionally outside the active interface. Larger deployments also need a scalable indexed search strategy, aggregate dashboard metrics, subcollection audit/quotation history, refund accounting, individual-ticket retention workflows and operational incident handling. Do not describe those as implemented features.
